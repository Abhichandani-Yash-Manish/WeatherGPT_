    Overview

    The national warning picture and the places you are working with.

    Warnings

    Official IMD district warning guidance, and the CAP relay reported separately.

    Map

    Official district geometry drawn from the vendored basemap. Click a district to inspect it.

    Observations

    Official station reports nearest the working place. An observation is not a forecast.

    Forecast

    Model forecast series for the working place, with the answering grid and its distance.

    Climate

    Published district rainfall records, 1901 to 2010, with per-year provenance.

    Advisories

    Published district farmer bulletins: pick a state and district, then read a passage with its saved page.

    Aviation

    Airport observations and terminal forecasts by ICAO code. Not a flight status or a clearance.

    Marine

    Modeled waves near a coastal place and modeled river discharge near a resolved place.

    Assistant

    Ask in your own words. Every answer keeps its source, its window and its retrieval time.
