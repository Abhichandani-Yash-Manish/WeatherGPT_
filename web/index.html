    What this answers

    Point forecasts for a named place or a pinned coordinate, with hourly rain probability, feels-like temperature, gusts and visibility.

    Published historical rainfall and national climate records, comparison and descriptive trend charts, and up to seven days of modeled daily reanalysis.

    Airport reports for supported ICAO codes, both METAR observations and TAF forecasts, with each report's own station and validity. Modeled wave height, direction and period near a coastal place, and modeled river discharge near a resolved place.

    Published district bulletins, including saved source pages you can open and download.

    Not connected: an observed water level or gauge reading, a tide or current, a live station observation for an arbitrary place, flood extent, official warning applicability, and road or travel clearance. Warning material is shown as reference unless a source states otherwise.
