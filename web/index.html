      Warnings

      Official IMD district warning guidance. The CAP relay is reported separately, and this workspace never issues a warning of its own.

      Map

      Official district geometry from the vendored basemap. Select a district to inspect it.

      Observations

      Station reports nearest the working place. An observation is a measurement already taken, not a forecast.

      Forecast

      Model forecast series for the working place, naming the grid cell that answered and how far away it is.

      What changed

      How stored retrievals of the same valid hour differ for the working place. This is vintage variance, not forecast skill, calibration or confidence.

      Climate records

      Published district rainfall records, 1901 to 2010, with per-year provenance.

      Farm advisories

      Published district farmer bulletins. Pick a state and district, then read a passage beside the page it came from.

      Aviation

      Airport observations and terminal forecasts by ICAO code. Not a flight status and not a clearance.

      Sea and rivers

      Modelled waves near a coastal place and modelled river discharge near a resolved place. Neither is an observed water level.

      Briefcase

      Briefs the workspace composed from its sources and kept. Nothing here is delivered, pushed or published; an export is a file you keep.

      Air quality

      CAMS modelled concentrations and the source’s own indices at a grid cell. Not a monitor measurement, not a health assessment and not an official warning.

      Ensemble spread

      One model’s returned members at a grid cell: mean, population spread, range and nearest-rank percentiles. Spread is not a probability, confidence, risk or skill measure.

      Forecast verification

      Archived model runs at fixed lead-time offsets, matched to ERA5 reanalysis. A measurement against reanalysis, not an observation-based skill score, and no model is ranked against another.

      Compare places

      Two or three places read side by side from the tray. Each column keeps its own timestamps and sources; nothing is ranked, averaged or differenced.

      Published documents

      Every edition this machine has indexed, with its printed issue date, measured currency and the state of its saved body. A stored document is the record of one printed edition, never a current warning.
