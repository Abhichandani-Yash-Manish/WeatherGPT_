 For proper nutrient management in transplanted rice, 
first top dressing of nitrogen fertilizer should be 
applied 30 –40 days after transplanting. Apply 32 kg of 
nitrogen per hectare for early -maturing varieties, 40 kg 
of nitrogen per hectare for medium to late -maturing 
varieties and 48 kg of nitrogen per hectare for late -
maturing varieties. 
 The rice yellow stem borer in rice can be effectively 
managed by installing five pheromone traps per hectare 
baited with lures that  attract the male moths. Spray 
neem-based insecticides by mixing 10 ml (5 EC) to 60 ml 
(0.03 EC) in 10 litres of water. Alternatively, apply 
Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 8 kg per acre or Fipronil 
0.3G with 10 kg per acre or Chlorantraniliprole 0.4G 
with 4 kg per acre after reducing the water level in the 
field. These granular insecticides should be applied 
twice, first application at the onset of pest infestation or 
30–35 days after transplanting and second application 
at 15 –20 days later. This schedule helps keep the pest 
infestation under control.
