The engine can ask a second time now: **docs/145** (`docs/145-asking-again-when-the-first-answer-was-nothing.md`), closing docs/144's last open item. The engine planned ONCE, before it had seen a single result, so a plan that was slightly wrong - a document family one notch too narrow, a district lookup for a question about a state - ended the turn telling the reader something was not held when it was the ASKING that was wrong. After a retrieval returns nothing, the model is now shown what it asked for and what each tool said, and gets ONE more attempt: either a new plan, through exactly the same validation and the same governed tools, or an empty task list meaning the absence is real. **Most of this batch is the second case.** An honest "the publisher has not issued this" is a CORRECT answer, and a retry loop is the obvious way to destroy that, so the prompt spends more words on when not to retry than on when to, and a second attempt may never become a different question - the reader's place is kept, never swapped or dropped to make a search succeed. The trigger is narrow: `unavailable`/`no_data`, nothing in facts, passages, nowcast rows, airport reports, warning or historical evidence, a plan that had tasks, and not a selection turn. `needs_clarification` and `needs_selection` are explicitly NOT retried - the reader has just been asked something and is owed the chance to answer. Four ways it cannot make a turn worse: exactly one attempt, an invalid second plan is dropped rather than retried again, a second retrieval that also finds nothing keeps the first answer (two honest absences are still one absence), and one that raises keeps it too. Adopting a second retrieval is disclosed in the answer. Measured: sea-surface temperature and the Ludhiana wheat gap both came back "the absence was judged real" - it declined to fish - while the Assam and Kerala flash-flood questions retried and answered. Fourteen tests, nine of them on when it must NOT fire. 1616→1630. Open: a cold cache made the first Assam retry 47.5s against ~10s warm, so warm the caches before a demo; the re-plan is model-planned and therefore not deterministic (an invalid second plan falls back safely); and the second retrieval is not run under a deadline, which would mean threading the turn loop.

The refresh is **resident** now, not scheduled: **docs/143** (`docs/143-the-refresh-that-never-stops.md`). `scripts/refresh_daemon.py` runs under a launchd agent with `RunAtLoad` and `KeepAlive` (`scripts/install_refresh_worker.sh`), so it is running whenever the machine is. It takes docs/142's queue one target at a time, pauses 20 seconds between them (about three requests a minute, all 698 districts in roughly four and a half hours), and goes IDLE once every district has been attempted today, waking every five minutes.
