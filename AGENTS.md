The source activation and document intake batch is `docs/29-source-activation-and-document-intake.md`. Two facts about a source stay separate and must not be merged again: `connected` means a registered connector ingests it, `wired_to_chat` means an ordinary conversation can reach it. Document intake keeps family, scope, region, physical page and printed issue evidence on every passage. Currency is measured from the printed issue date against the retrieval date and stays unknown when the layout does not state one — retrieval success is never an issue date, and a district serving a byte-identical PDF for three days is the reason. The district family is target-driven from the publisher's 698-entry directory; district ids are nationally unique and a directory refresh introducing a repeat is refused rather than resolved by guessing. The publisher supports no conditional request (HEAD refused, no validator headers, `If-Modified-Since` and `Range` ignored), so every target is downloaded and its sha256 is the change signal; this is measured, not assumed, and re-measuring is cheaper than trusting it. Outcomes stay distinct: `not_issued` is an answer, `layout_unrecognised` is held, an OCR page is quarantined, and only transport or extraction errors are failures. Failure records are preserved. Document bodies are pruned after seven days while passages, hashes and manifests are kept permanently, and `/api/documents/<sha>` answers 410 naming what survives rather than 404 denying the document. Family markers come from sampled real front pages and each names the district it came from; a marker list fitted to a couple of failures is not evidence. The corpus is indexed but not yet reachable from chat — the chat path is still the narrow chunk reader, and that is the open A06 work.
