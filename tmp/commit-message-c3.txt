C3: let the reader choose how much of an answer to show

The register switch sits above the transcript - Brief, Conversational, Full evidence - and sets a
data-register attribute on the thread itself, so it applies to every card already on screen without
re-rendering a turn.

- brief hides the evidence apparatus (ruler, receipt, disclosures, product comparison); conversational is
  the renderer's own card; full evidence opens every disclosure in the thread and restores the disclosures
  exactly as the renderer built them when the reader leaves that register.
- the choice is remembered per browser, and an unreadable or absent stored value falls back to
  conversational instead of being guessed at.
- it deliberately does not re-order or re-word a card: a component check asserts the card text is identical
  before and after the switch moves. Moving a fact away from its label is where a number can lose its
  entity, window or source, so the reading order stays the renderer's.

The README per-suite counts are now the checks each suite actually prints, measured rather than carried.

Tests: tests/test_views.js pins the register behaviour and the unchanged text; tests/test_conversation_ui.js
pins the wiring, the pressed state, the remembered choice and the fallback. 1183 Python tests, 10 component
suites (107 printed checks), 27 verification steps, none failing.
