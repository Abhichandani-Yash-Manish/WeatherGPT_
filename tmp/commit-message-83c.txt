A cheap first look, a frozen provider, and a written answer for a turn with facts

(a) The routing tier. A short call now asks one question - conversation or task - against a ~700-character
workspace picture, and answers a conversation in that same call. A task still goes to the full planner, so no
plan is made more cheaply than before; an undecided route is a task; a first look that fails or exceeds its
12 s budget changes nothing; and the plan it builds is validated like any other. Measured on the same
afternoon and provider order: "hello" 11.0 -> 4.2 s, "what can you do?" 19.3 -> 4.2 s, "17 times 3" 48.3 ->
8.0 s (and it now answers 51 as general knowledge), "नमस्ते" 9.3 -> 10.1 s. The measured cost: a task turn
pays for the first look too, about 3-4 s, and the switch can be turned off per workspace.

(c) The provider policy is frozen to the free cloud models. WEATHERGPT_PROVIDERS=cloud_free is the default:
the curated free OpenRouter ids only, ordered most capable first; the local model runs only when the policy
asks for it and is not a fallback that runs by itself; with no key there is no provider at all. The policy is
recorded in every planning and composition trace, and ModelRouter.state() reports the policy, the planner
policy, the first-look switch and each provider's availability.

(b) Stage A3. A turn with facts now gets a written two-or-three-sentence answer from those facts, behind the
checks that already covered model text, extended with the fact unit set, the required fact place, and a 25 s
wall-clock budget for the whole composition step. Any failure - a changed value, a changed unit, a lost place,
a missing evidence id, a link, a certainty, a slow or refused call - leaves the tool-owned renderer stating the
facts, with the refusal recorded in trace.generation. The fact rows, ruler and receipt stay tool-owned. The
claim-attribution test now exercises exactly that: the model may write the answer, and it may not rewrite what
the facts say.

Hardening: whole-step provider budgets (45 s planning, 25 s composition, 12 s first look) replace per-attempt
timeouts, so a refused free endpoint reaches the reader with an honest error in about a minute instead of a
two-minute retry loop.

Measured incident, recorded not hidden (research/reviews/chat-overhaul-20260916/router-tier-and-quota.json):
at 17:52 UTC every cloud-free call was refused - "HTTP 429 Rate limit exceeded: free-models-per-day". With the
cloud-only policy frozen and nothing else enabled, a turn says "No model provider could answer" and holds its
facts. Options are recorded, not chosen: wait for the daily reset, add a small credit, lower the request count
with WEATHERGPT_ROUTER=off, or unfreeze the local model.

check reaches a provider. 1204 Python tests, 10 component suites (108 printed checks), 27 verification steps,
none failing.
