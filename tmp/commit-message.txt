C1: say what the question was read as, and open on the conversation

The front door now answers the first thing a reader asks of a slow engine - "what did it hear?" - while
the real turn is still retrieving, and a fresh visit lands on Ask rather than the command centre.

- POST /api/chat/preview (chat-preview-v1) plans with the deterministic rules only: the same rule
  planner and plan-settling path the real turn uses, so a reading cannot contradict the engine own
  vocabulary. It calls no model, reads no source, takes no place in the bounded wait queue, binds no
  conversation and saves no turn, so a preview can never appear in history, satisfy a follow-up or be
  mistaken for an answer.
- a reading names the place, the window with its IST boundaries, the measures and the products that will
  be read, carries provisional:true and publishes no value, citation, status or expiry. Measured live on
  the real store: 0.1 s for a recognised question, and 1244 conversations before and after.
- the rules refuse a bare continuation ("and tomorrow?") because only the model planner can see what it
  continues, so the preview reports what the conversation is still carrying instead of guessing at a
  reading it cannot make, and labels it as carried.
- the working placeholder shows the reading and the conversation own receipt (place and server answer
  time, cleared with a new conversation); the answer card replaces the whole box.
- a fresh visit with no route opens Ask, every route still opens the surface it names, an unknown route
  falls back to Ask, and the rail keeps every guided surface with the hint that names the view it opens.

docs/82-chatbot-experience.md records the four decisions, this C1 slice and the planned C2 (measured
continuity journeys) and C3 (reader-chosen register), with what C1 deliberately does not do: it states
the reading, not held values, because no read model exposes a held value with the provenance and validity
that printing one would need.

Tests: tests/test_chat_preview.py (8 checks) and the token-gated HTTP route in tests/test_workspace.py
pin the backend contract; tests/test_views.js pins the provisional reading and the receipt, and
tests/test_suite_ui.js pins the front door. 1176 Python tests, 10 component suites, 27 verification steps,
none failing.
