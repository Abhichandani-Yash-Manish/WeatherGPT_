A5: who answers, in what order, and what failed last, on the settings surface

The reader could see which provider answered only inside a card's trace disclosure, and the reason for a
refusal only in an error message. The settings surface now states it.

- ModelRouter.state() reports the provider policy, the planner policy, the first-look switch, every provider
  with its availability and model, and the last failure. The router records a failure per refused client and,
  when none answers, the whole chain with its reason and time.
- /api/settings/capabilities carries that state, and the Model providers card renders the policy in force, the
  planner policy, the first look, each provider, the free ids behind them, the refused ids and the last failure.
- scripts/models.py --set-key now takes openrouter (default) or deepseek, so the key command printed in the
  workspace is one that actually runs.

Tests: tests/test_provider_visibility.py (4 checks) for the payload, the recorded failure, the environment
policy and the key storage, and a settings-card assertion in tests/test_suite_ui.js. 1214 Python tests, 10
component suites (109 printed checks), 27 verification steps, none failing.
