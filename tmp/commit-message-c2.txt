C2 part: offer only next questions the rules can read, and say what a continuation carried

The continuity work started with a measurement rather than a design: the rules floor reads a forecast
question when it names a day AND a part of day, and refuses one that names only the day (twelve of twelve
shapes tested). The local model planner was not reachable while this ran - http://127.0.0.1:11434 refused
the connection - so the four continuity journeys are recorded as not measured, and nothing here depends on
a model call.

- next-question chips are built from the plan own day and part of day, and every candidate reply is planned
  by the rules at offer time; a shape they refuse is dropped rather than shown as a promise. A chip names a
  place and a day only - never a value, a probability, a warning or a source this turn did not read - and it
  uses the plan own place name, because the gazetteer label can read as several places ("Surat, Surat, State
  of Gujarat" read as two).
- measured live on the real store: a rules-planned answer in 0.148 s offered three chips, all three readable
  by the rules and each resolving to one place.
- a continuation discloses what it carried and what the person changed, from the plan own context_action and
  changed_fields, and labels it as context kept rather than new evidence. A fresh question gets no line.

The journeys themselves stay open: measuring them end-to-end needs a reachable model planner, and the batch
says so instead of assuming it.
