The model plans every turn, and a conversation is a real answer

Measured first: "hello" used to end the turn with a validation error, because a greeting has no task to
quote, and "what can you do?" was answered from the model's memory of itself in 28.9 s. The planner also
silently preferred the deterministic rules and died rather than degrading when a model plan did not validate.

- ModelRouter plans with the provider chain by default. The deterministic rules plan only when
  WEATHERGPT_PLANNER=rules, and trace.planning.planner_policy records which policy answered each turn. A
  provider outage names every provider tried and is never quietly answered from the rules floor.
- a plan that cannot be validated is repaired with the validator's own message, and a turn that still cannot
  be planned degrades to a readable answer with the reason in the trace instead of raising at the reader.
- a new chat task kind carries the model's own reply for greetings, thank-yous, capability and meta questions,
  general reasoning and out-of-scope messages. No tool runs and nothing is acquired.
- the planner receives a deterministic workspace picture - the clock, the callable tools, the source ledger
  counts and what this workspace does not do - so a capability answer comes from the ledger, not from memory.
- a conversational reply is checked for measurement units (including spelled-out forms), dates and times,
  numbers beside a weather word, warning claims, source identifiers, links and present-weather claims; one
  repair attempt is made with the violation named, and a reply that still fails is withheld.
- a conversational turn holds no fact, cites nothing, carries a visible "No source read" tag on the card, and
  never becomes retrievable evidence in the conversation state.
- the language tier now labels a model-written answer in the requested script as written_in_requested_script
  instead of claiming a template wrote it.

Live journeys (OpenRouter free first, local Ollama second; recorded in
research/reviews/chat-overhaul-20260916/journeys.json): "hello" 11.0 s conversation; "what can you do?" 19.3 s
naming the real tools and limits; "what is 17 times 3?" 48.3 s answering fifty-one as general knowledge;
"नमस्ते" 9.3 s in Devanagari; "Will it rain in Surat tomorrow morning?" 19.5 s answered with one tool-owned
fact (0.4 mm, GFS) and three quick replies; "thanks!" in the same conversation 19.5 s. No turn needed a repair.

The leak check is a bounded heuristic and docs/83 says so: a number written as a word passes it. What a reader
can rely on is the structure - a conversational turn acquires nothing, holds no fact, is labelled and is never
stored as evidence. Latency (9-48 s on the free provider) is the open cost.
